Bridging Basics

1. ETH is the project's home chain.

2. When you bridge from ETH, your tokens are locked in the bridge contract.

3. The same number of tokens that you locked are minted on the new chain.

4. The tokens on the new chain can be traded on that chain until someone bridges them back to ETH.

5. At that point, the tokens on the new chain are burned, and the ETH tokens are unlocked from the bridge contract.

1. Minting Mechanics

Iced Ether is not minted out of thin air—each newly minted token is backed by the ETH used to mint it. 68.6% of the ETH used to mint is converted to StETH and stored in the Iceberg. 31.4% goes directly to the LP.

Only 3.14% of the total supply can be minted in a 24-hour period to prevent continuous supply inflation, enforced with a 31.4-hour timelock.

2. Avalanche Rebalance

The primary purpose of the Avalanche mechanism is to rebalance the LP. An Avalanche triggers when ETH in the LP reaches 31.4% of StETH in the Iceberg.

When triggered: 9.86% of StETH in the Iceberg converts to ETH and feeds the LP, while 21.55% of all tokens in the LP are permanently burned.

3. Yield Management & Burning

Yield from StETH accumulates in the Iceberg until a threshold of .314 ETH is hit, melting the yield directly into the LP.

Due to fluctuating gas costs, tokens are burned daily from the LP equal to the yield generated, preserving maximum value without high gas overhead.

Dual Liquidity Pool Architecture

Iced Ether utilizes two connected pools: the Mainnet Liquidity Pool (LP) and the Iceberg StETH Reserve Vault.

  • 68.6% of all deposited minting ETH is converted to stETH and held in the Iceberg Vault.
  • 31.4% of deposited minting ETH is sent directly to the Liquidity Pool.
  • 3.14% transaction tax on all swaps and transfers is routed straight into the Iceberg.

Avalanche Rebalancing System

When ETH in the LP reaches 31.4% of the stETH balance in the Iceberg, the automated Avalanche mechanism triggers to rebalance liquidity.

  • 9.86% of Iceberg stETH is converted to ETH and injected directly into the LP.
  • 21.55% of all $ICED tokens in the Liquidity Pool are permanently burned.
  • Execution is distributed across 12 Fibonacci steps over 31.4 hours (9,420 blocks) to eliminate MEV sandwich attacks.
⚖️

Minting Rules & Timelocks

Tokens cannot be hyper-inflated. Minting is capped at 3.14% of Total Supply per 24 hours.

  • Newly minted tokens carry a strict 31.4-hour timelock before they can be transferred or swapped.
  • Minters receive a waived 3.14% swap tax plus a 3.14% bonus token allocation.
  • Tokens are backed 1:1 by real stETH and LP reserves.

Yield Management & Gas-Optimized LP Burning

StETH in the Iceberg continuously accumulates yield. Once 0.314 ETH in yield is generated, it melts directly into the LP.

To save gas during high Ethereum mainnet congestion, tokens equal to daily generated yield are burned systematically directly from the LP, achieving the exact same deflationary effect without excessive mainnet transaction fees.

Cross-Chain Bridging Architecture

Ethereum Mainnet serves as the protocol's home chain.

When tokens are bridged to external chains (Polygon, Base, BNB, Arbitrum), mainnet tokens are locked in the bridge contract while wrapped tokens are minted 1:1 on the target chain. When bridging back, destination tokens are burned to unlock native mainnet tokens.
